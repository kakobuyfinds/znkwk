How to use the HipoBuy spreadsheet

Confirm the item and size

Open the product listing and check its item ID, color, size chart, stock, and current price. For clothing, compare the seller’s flat measurements with a garment you already own.

Check the total cost

Add domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes to the item price. Ask how the shipping line calculates billable weight, especially if you plan to keep bulky packaging.

Review QC photos before dispatch

If your agent provides warehouse photos, match the color and size label to your order. Check stitching, soles or prints as appropriate, and review the return window before approving dispatch.

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping directory that groups product links by category, so you can compare listings and build a shortlist before choosing what to order.

Are the USD prices the final amount I pay?

The cards show approximate conversions of listed item prices from CNY. Your total depends on the variant, the exchange rate used at checkout, service fees, shipping, and taxes.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check your shopping agent’s warehouse or order page once the item arrives. Photo availability and the cost of additional views depend on the agent and order.

Where do the product links take me?

Product cards open their matching HipobuyFind listings. The two buttons at the top open the HipobuyFind homepage.
